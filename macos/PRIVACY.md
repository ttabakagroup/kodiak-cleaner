# Kodiak Cleaner for Mac Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026

Kodiak Cleaner for Mac is made by Ted Tabaka, an independent developer. This policy explains exactly what it does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your Mac.

## What Stays on Your Mac

- Your settings are saved in `~/Library/Application Support/Kodiak Cleaner/Settings.json`.
- Triggers History and its backup copies are saved in `~/Library/Application Support/Kodiak Cleaner/Backups`.
- The Cleaner's history (when each item was last cleaned and how much it freed) is saved with your settings.
- How many times Kodiak Cleaner has started is saved with your settings, for the support reminder. It is never sent anywhere.
- What the Cleaner and Triggers pages find is shown to you and never saved anywhere else.

## What Is Never Collected

Kodiak Cleaner for Mac has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, ads or accounts. It never sends anything about you, your Mac or what it finds.

## Going Online

Kodiak Cleaner for Mac never goes online by itself. When you click a link (such as Website, Source Code, Ko-fi or Star on GitHub), it opens in your own browser. Contact & Feedback opens the contact form on the Kodiak Cleaner website in your own browser, with the topic and the Kodiak Cleaner version filled in; nothing is sent until you send it there.

## Administrator Rights

Cleaning never needs administrator rights. Turning a launch daemon off or on does, and Drive Wiper may when macOS needs it to erase a drive: Kodiak Cleaner asks through macOS's own password prompt each time and never sees or keeps your password.

## Full Disk Access

macOS keeps Safari, Mail and the Trash private. If you want Kodiak Cleaner to clean them, you can allow it under Full Disk Access in System Settings, Privacy & Security. Kodiak Cleaner only uses that access to clean the items you tick, and you can take it away there at any time.

## Supporter Codes

Supporter codes are checked on your Mac against a key built into Kodiak Cleaner. The code is saved in your settings file and never sent anywhere.

## Changes to This Policy

A new version of Kodiak Cleaner for Mac may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
