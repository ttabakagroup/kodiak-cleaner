## Built Around What Matters.

For more than two decades, I used the same cleaning app. It was familiar, straightforward, and did exactly what I needed. When it was acquired, I was disappointed, but I remained hopeful that it would retain the simplicity I had come to rely on.

The latest upgrade changed that. Familiar features became harder to find, and routine tasks required more effort than they should. An app I had trusted for years no longer felt like the one I had chosen. That frustration inspired me to build Kodiak Cleaner, an open source application focused on simplicity, privacy, and practical functionality.

The idea is straightforward: open the app, choose what you want to clean, complete the task, and get on with your day. A cleaning tool should respect your time, with clear controls, intuitive navigation, and no unnecessary delays.

After nearly 30 years in information technology, I had also grown tired of switching between multiple tools to complete recurring tasks. I wanted to bring those capabilities together in one application to make everyday maintenance more efficient and convenient.

Privacy and security are central to that vision. My goal is for Kodiak Cleaner to prioritize local processing and user privacy, with security built in from the beginning. You should feel confident in how an application handles your device and data.

I also believe simplicity can feel modern. A fresh interface, clear options, and customizable color schemes can give software personality while keeping everyday tasks easy.

Making Kodiak Cleaner open source is an invitation to help shape its future. I welcome feedback, guidance, and contributions, from feature suggestions to bug reports and code improvements. My goal is to develop the app collaboratively while staying focused on what makes it useful.

This project began with an unwelcome change and became an opportunity to build something I had long wanted: a simple, private, practical cleaning app that I hope others will find useful.

If you find Kodiak Cleaner helpful, please consider making a donation to support its continued development and maintenance. Although I originally built it for my own use, preparing it for others has required considerable time, care, and effort. I am committed to improving its reliability, addressing bugs, and learning along the way, and I welcome anyone who wants to help make it better.
