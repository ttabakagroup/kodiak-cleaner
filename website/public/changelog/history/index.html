0.8.121 Dev

October 25, 2026

About

  • Your Plan no longer reads "Free · unlocked by: None". On the free plan it just says Free.

Drive Wiper

  • Safer drive protection: if Kodiak Cleaner can't tell which disk holds Windows, Kodiak Cleaner itself, your user folder, your programs or a page file, or which disk Windows starts from, no drive can be erased, and each drive in the list says why.
  • A wipe that stops partway because a drive was unplugged or couldn't be written now says so honestly: part of the drive was overwritten and its partitions are gone, so wipe it again to finish. It's written to the activity log too. The drive is set up again only when it's still attached as the same drive.
  • The DoD 3 Passes hint only says the last pass is read back when it really is: on an Entire Drive wipe with Verify the Last Pass on.
  • Drive names keep to the letters A to Z, numbers, spaces, hyphens and underscores, so they come out right on every PC. A line under the name box says so.
  • The tick box next to Drive Name now reads Name the Drive.
  • A drive that doesn't report a model is listed as "Disk 0: Unnamed Drive" instead of "Disk 0: Disk 0".
  • The Letters column is wide enough for No Drive Letter.
  • If reading the drives fails in an unexpected way, the page now shows what went wrong instead of staying on "Reading drives…".

General

  • When Kodiak Cleaner runs as administrator, links to the website, Ko-fi and Contact & Feedback open your browser normally, without administrator rights.
  • Errors in background tasks are now written to the activity log.

Getting Started

  • The Hash Checker line now lists SHA-384 too.

Support

  • The support reminder now says every tool stays free, which is accurate: a few color schemes are a thank-you for Supporters.

Drive Wiper

  • The progress card has a small X in its upper-right corner once a wipe or drive check has finished, stopped or failed. It hides the card and the divider below it; the card comes back with the next wipe or check. While a wipe or check is running, the X is hidden and Stop stays.

0.8.116 Dev

October 24, 2026

Drive Wiper

  • New: Name the Drive, a tick box next to Drive Name. It's ticked by default and the drive gets the name in the box. Untick it and the box goes blank: the drive is formatted with no name, and Windows shows it as USB Drive or Local Disk.
  • The suggested name is no longer "Wiped". It follows the type of drive you tick: Flash Drive, Hard Drive, USB Drive, SSD or USB SSD, short enough for every file system. Type your own and it's kept. Names on FAT32 drives are shown in capitals, the way Windows stores them.
  • New defaults: Wipe Type is Quick, and Leave It Uninitialized Like a New Drive and Check the Drive Afterwards are off. Turn them on when you need them.
  • The drive list in Step 1 grows to show every drive (up to about ten before it scrolls), and the mouse wheel over it scrolls the whole page instead of getting stuck in the list.
  • Click anywhere on a drive's row to tick or untick it.
  • When two or more drives are ticked, a warning says how many will be erased, in Step 1 and next to the Wipe button.
  • New: Drive Details. The info button at the right of each drive opens a summary: model, serial number, type, connection, size, firmware version, partition style and partitions, the drive's own health report (health and status, temperature, wear, hours powered on, read and write errors, and replaced, pending and unreadable sectors where the drive shares them), and the disk errors Windows logged for it in the last 30 days, with a quick verdict. Copy puts it on the clipboard. It only reads; nothing is written to the drive.

0.8.115 Dev

October 24, 2026

Drive Wiper

  • Entire Drive is now laid out in three numbered steps: 1. Select the Drive (the drive list and Refresh), 2. Wipe and Format (Wipe Type, Security, Verify the Last Pass, and the format with its recommendation), 3. After the Wipe (Leave It Uninitialized Like a New Drive, Check the Drive Afterwards), then the warning and the Wipe and Check Drive buttons. Free Space Only keeps its simple layout.
  • "Firmware Erase" is now called "Built-In Secure Erase", because it's the drive's own erase command: it wipes the data, not the drive's firmware, and the drive keeps working afterwards. The Method column, progress, results and activity log use the new name, and hovering a drive's method says what it does.
  • How Each Drive Type Is Erased is rewritten in plain words, with a note that every drive keeps working afterwards and is set up again as chosen in Step 3. It shows with Entire Drive, the only mode that erases whole drives.
  • New: Open Reports opens the Reports\Integrity Check folder, where every drive check is saved. It's in the progress section once a check finishes, and next to Check Drive.
  • New: a draggable divider between the progress section and the settings, like the one on the Startup page. Drag it to give the drive check results or the settings more room; it's remembered, and a double-click puts it back.

Integrity Check

  • New: Open Reports beside the last run's details, so a check's full report is one click away. The button at the top is now called Open Reports too.

0.8.114 Dev

October 24, 2026

Drive Wiper

  • New: Check the Drive Afterwards (on by default, Entire Drive only). After the wipe and setup, each drive gets a health check, so you know whether it can be trusted with data again.
  • New: Check Drive button next to Wipe. It checks the ticked drives without erasing anything: the drive is only read, never written.
  • The check reads the drive's own health report through Windows (health and status, read and write errors it corrected and couldn't correct, wear on SSDs, temperature, hours powered on, and replaced, pending and unreadable sectors where the drive shares them), the disk errors Windows logged for that drive in the last 30 days, and then every sector of the drive, noting areas that can't be read or read unusually slowly.
  • When the wipe already read its last pass back (Verify the Last Pass), that write-and-read-back counts as the surface test and the drive isn't read a second time; the result says so.
  • Each drive gets a plain verdict, Healthy, Warning or Failing, with the reasons ("3 unreadable areas: this drive is failing; don't trust it with data") and the values behind them. Progress, the time left and Stop show in the progress section at the top of the page.
  • Each check is written to the activity log and saved as a report in Reports\Integrity Check. Checking needs administrator rights; without them Kodiak Cleaner offers to restart as administrator.

  • Fixed: erasing an entire USB flash drive could leave it unformatted. 0.8.0 Dev

October 4, 2026

Cleaning

  • Cleaner for Windows temporary files, the Recycle Bin, thumbnail cache, error reports, crash dumps, DirectX shader cache, Windows Update and Delivery Optimization caches and the recent documents list.
  • Browser cleaning (cache, history, cookies) for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Firefox, skipped while the browser is open.
  • Analyze first to see sizes, then run the cleaner. Your selections are remembered.
  • Registry scanner that backs up every fix to a .reg file before making it.
  • Startup manager that turns programs on and off the same way Task Manager does.
  • Installed programs list with search and uninstall.

Tools

  • Duplicate Finder: finds files with identical contents. Removed copies go to the Recycle Bin, and the last copy is never removed.
  • System Restore: list, create and remove Windows restore points.
  • Drive Wiper: overwrite free space, or securely erase a whole non-system drive.

Settings

  • Options & Settings with its own sidebar: General, Cleaning, Updates and Advanced, then Appearance, Supporter, About, Backups, Contact & Feedback and Terms of Use.
  • Every setting is a switch, saved straight away to KodiakCleaner.ini next to KodiakCleaner.exe. Nothing is stored in AppData.
  • Honey (warm cream and gold) and Glacier (calm blue-grey) color schemes, each in light and dark, following Windows by default.
  • About page with the version and channel, your plan, what's new, a privacy summary and credits.
  • Contact & Feedback opens a message in your own email app; nothing is sent automatically.
  • Supporter page: a one-time thank-you on Ko-fi unlocks the Glacier theme and a Supporter Badge. Codes are checked on your PC only.
  • Built-in Terms of Use and Privacy Policy that work offline.
  • Getting Started window on first launch, and What's New after each update.

Install

  • MSI installer and portable ZIP. Dev, Beta and Stable builds install side by side.
  • Optional update check against GitHub releases (off by default), with each download verified against its published SHA-256.
