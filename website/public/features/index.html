Programs

  • Searchable list of installed programs, each with its own icon.
  • Uninstall several at once. Each one gets a registry backup, its own uninstaller, then a list of leftovers to remove.
  • Waits until an uninstaller has completely finished, including ones that hand over to a helper.
  • Repair, Rename and Delete Entry for broken or confusing listings (backed up first).
  • History records everything removed or changed, and Restore Selected puts it back where it can.

Registry

Finds entries that point at files or programs that no longer exist. Fifteen checks, all ticked to start:

ActiveX and Class IssuesApplication PathsApplicationsFontsHelp FilesInstallerMissing Shared DLLsMUI CacheObsolete SoftwareRun At StartupSound EventsStart Menu OrderingType LibrariesUnused File ExtensionsWindows Services
  • A Type column shows which check found each entry.
  • Fix everything, a selection, or one entry with a right-click.
  • Every fix is saved to a .reg backup first. Double-click a backup to undo.
  • Only reports an entry when the file or folder it points to is definitely gone from a local drive. Files in the Windows folder, drivers and core Windows services are never reported.
  • Entries that Windows itself protects are left alone, and 32-bit programs in Program Files (x86) are never reported as missing.

Scheduled Cleaning

  • Named cleanings, such as your browsers every week and Windows once a month.
  • Pick weekdays or a day of the month, a time, and the items.
  • Windows Task Scheduler starts Kodiak Cleaner, which cleans without a window and closes. Nothing runs in the background.
  • A cleaning missed while the PC was off runs at the next chance.
  • Items with a caution can't be scheduled.
New Schedule

Startup

  • Programs that start when you sign in, per user and for all users, each with its icon.
  • Turn them off or on one at a time or in a batch, just like Task Manager.
  • Add a program or shortcut to your Startup folder.
  • Removed entries are backed up first, and History can restore them, on or off as they were.

Boot TimerNew

  • Restart from Kodiak Cleaner and see how long shutting down, Windows starting and the desktop settling took.
  • A timeline and your recent restarts side by side, so you can tell whether a change helped.
  • What Slowed the Start lists the startup programs, drivers and services that held things up, slowest first.
  • Show in Startup and Turn Off at Startup, right from the list.
Kodiak Cleaner

Browser ExtensionsNew

  • Every extension in Ablaze Floorp, Brave, Google Chrome, Helium, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Mullvad Browser and Vivaldi, with its icon, version and state.
  • Turn an extension on or off with its switch, or right-click to remove it. Ctrl-click and Shift-click pick several.
  • Chrome, Edge and Brave are changed through their own extension policies, the supported way to manage a browser, and the policy is removed again once nothing needs it.
  • Firefox-based add-ons can be removed while that browser is closed.
Kodiak Cleaner

Defrag and TRIMNew

  • Lists your drives with their type, file system, size and use.
  • Hard drives get Defragment, SSD and NVMe drives get TRIM, and flash drives are left alone.
  • Everything that needs it is ticked, so one click optimizes every drive the right way.
  • Analyze shows how fragmented hard drives are. Uses Windows' own optimizer.
Kodiak Cleaner

Disk AnalyzerNew

  • Analyze All Drives, the drives you tick (network drives too) or one folder.
  • A map of every folder and file, sized by the space it takes and colored by kind of file in shades of your own accent color.
  • Hover a box for its size and path, click a folder to look inside, and go back with Backspace or the mouse's back button.
  • Contents and Largest Files lists, and a right-click menu to Copy Path, Move to Recycle Bin or Show in File Explorer.
  • Fast scan: run as administrator and whole NTFS drives are read straight from the drive's own file table, many times faster on a full drive.
Kodiak Cleaner

Drive WiperNew

  • Overwrite free space so deleted files can't be recovered, or erase whole drives you're giving away.
  • Each drive's type is detected and wiped the right way: 1 Pass, DoD 3 or DoD 7 passes, or 30 passes for hard drives and flash drives, and the drive's own firmware erase for SSD and NVMe drives.
  • Wipe Type: Quick, Thorough or Secure, with a read-back check of the last pass.
  • Leave it uninitialized like a new drive, or format it as GPT or MBR with NTFS, exFAT or FAT32.
  • Three checks before anything starts, and the drives holding Windows, Kodiak Cleaner, your user folder or programs can't be ticked.
Kodiak Cleaner

Driver CleanerNew

  • Removes an old AMD, Intel or NVIDIA graphics driver, or a Realtek audio driver, completely before a fresh install.
  • A restore point and a backup of each driver package come first. Registry keys are backed up and folders go to the Recycle Bin.
  • Restart in Safe Mode for the cleanest removal, and a switch stops Windows Update putting the old driver back.
  • Get the Latest Driver opens the maker's own download page.
Kodiak Cleaner

Duplicate FinderNew

  • Finds identical files in folders or whole drives (size, quick hash, then full SHA-256). The last copy is never removed.
  • In-Depth mode finds files that are alike, such as edited copies, re-saved pictures and older drafts, ranked by how alike they are.
  • Match by similar names, leaving out copy marks, version words, dates or your own words, or type a name pattern such as "Invoice #.pdf".
  • Verify Hashes confirms each set again before you remove anything.
Kodiak Cleaner

Empty FoldersNew

  • Search whole drives, one or several, or tick All Drives, as well as folders.
  • Each drive shows its free space and a bar.
  • Windows, program and AppData folders are never searched, and your Desktop, Documents and other Windows folders are never removed.
  • Removed folders go to the Recycle Bin.
Kodiak Cleaner

Integrity CheckNew

  • Check and repair Windows and your drives with Windows' own tools. Check Everything runs every check without changing anything.
  • Windows System Files, the Windows Component Store and Drive File System, each with Repair where Windows can fix it.
  • Drive Health and Drive Error History in plain words, and a Memory Test that runs at the next restart.
  • Windows' own messages are kept under Details and in Reports.
Kodiak Cleaner

Large Files

  • Lists the biggest files in a folder (50 MB and up, your choice).
  • Windows, program folders and hidden or system files are skipped.
  • Removed files go to the Recycle Bin.
Kodiak Cleaner

Mass RenameNew

  • Rename many files at once by a pattern: the old name, a counting number, the folder name, and the date created, modified or taken.
  • Find and Replace (with regular expressions), letter case, extension and tidy spaces.
  • Every new name is shown beside the old one first, and names that would clash are flagged and kept.
  • Undo Last Rename puts the names back, even after Kodiak Cleaner is closed and opened again.
Kodiak Cleaner

Scheduled TasksNew

  • Lists the programs Windows runs on a schedule and turns them off or back on. Tasks are never deleted.
  • A Program column shows what each task runs, with its icon. Click it to see the file in File Explorer.
  • Hover over a task to see its command, publisher, author and description.
  • Windows' own tasks are hidden unless you ask.

System RestoreNew

  • Lists, creates and removes restore points, and opens System Restore to roll back. Create Restore Point always makes a new point, even when Windows made one that day.
  • Point-in-Time Restore (Windows 11 24H2 and later): turn it on, choose how often Windows takes a snapshot and how long it keeps them, Snapshot Now, and Restart to Recovery to restore.
  • Settings are backed up before each change.

Safety

Built-In Safety

  • Browsers are skipped while they're open, and files in use are skipped.
  • Cleaning folders are sanity-checked before anything is deleted.
  • Kodiak Cleaner runs as a normal user and asks for administrator rights only for system-wide items.
  • Program changes, registry fixes and startup removals are backed up first, and can be restored.
  • Risky Advanced Cleaning items carry a caution, ask before they're ticked, and never run on a schedule.
  • Symbolic links and junctions are never followed.
  • Every pop-up puts the safe choice first, so pressing Enter never does the risky thing.
  • Uninstalling leaves nothing behind, and asks before deleting your backups, logs and reports.

Privacy

Private and Local First

Nothing Collected

No telemetry, analytics, crash reports, accounts or ads. Nothing is sent automatically, ever.

Your Files Stay Yours

Settings, backups and the optional activity log are plain files on your own PC: in Documents\Kodiak Cleaner for the installed version, or beside the program for the portable one. Never in the cloud.

Updates Only When Asked

Kodiak Cleaner only goes online to check for updates, when you click Check Now or turn on automatic updates.

Feedback When You Choose

The feedback buttons in the app open the Contact Us form on this website. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Docs Folder Included

The installer, the portable ZIP and the Mac and Linux downloads each come with a Docs folder: Changelog, Download, Homepage, License, Privacy, ReadMe and Terms of Use, all in plain text.
