How It Works

Open It, Clean It, Get On With Your Day

Kodiak Cleaner shows you what it found before anything is removed, so you always stay in charge.

Analyze

Tick what you want to clean and click Analyze. Kodiak Cleaner measures how much each item would free. Nothing is deleted yet.

Review

Check the results, sort by size or by when each item was last cleaned, and untick anything you'd rather keep.

Clean

Click Run Cleaner. Browsers that are open and files in use are skipped, so nothing important is pulled out from under you.

Why People Choose It

Every registry fix, startup removal and program entry change is backed up first, and most removals go to the Recycle Bin.

Clean Windows and Every Browser You Use

Programs

Uninstall Cleanly, Leftovers and All

Kodiak Cleaner backs up a program's registry entries, runs its own uninstaller, waits until it has truly finished, then offers to remove the folders, shortcuts and registry keys it left behind.

  • Uninstall several programs, one at a time.
  • Repair, Rename or Delete Entry for broken listings.
  • History records every change and can restore it.

Linux

Kodiak Cleaner for Linux

The Linux edition cleans system junk, browser data, package manager downloads and program caches, and its Triggers page lists everything that starts automatically: autostart entries, systemd services and timers, cron jobs and startup scripts.

  • DEB package for Debian, Ubuntu and Mint, or an AppImage for other distributions.
  • The same color schemes, and the same supporter code.
  • Every change to a trigger is backed up and can be restored.

Mac

Kodiak Cleaner for Mac

A native Mac app for macOS 26 or newer. It cleans macOS junk, browser data and developer tool caches, and its Triggers page lists launch agents, launch daemons, cron jobs and shell startup files.

  • A disk image or a ZIP, for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  • No network code at all. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  • The same color schemes, and the same supporter code.

Why It Was Made

For more than two decades, I used the same cleaning app. The latest upgrade changed that.

Routine tasks took more effort and familiar features got harder to find, so Ted built his own cleaner and made it open source: a simple, private and practical tool that respects your time.

Privacy

Everything Stays on Your PC

  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reports, accounts or ads. Nothing is sent automatically, ever.
  • Settings and backups are plain files on your own PC.
  • Updates are the only network use, and only when you click Check Now or turn on automatic updates (off by default).
  • Feedback opens a message in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
