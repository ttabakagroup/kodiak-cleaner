What Kodiak Cleaner Does

Kodiak Cleaner deletes temporary files, browser data and other items you choose, fixes registry entries you select, turns startup programs and scheduled tasks off or on, deletes the scheduled tasks you pick, uninstalls, repairs or renames programs and removes the leftovers you pick after an uninstall, removes the drivers you pick, renames many files at once, removes duplicate files, large files and empty folders you pick, manages System Restore points, and can overwrite free space or erase a whole drive. Some of these actions can't be undone.

  • Review what Kodiak Cleaner shows you before you clean, fix, remove or wipe anything.
  • Registry fixes are backed up to a .reg file first unless you choose otherwise. Scheduled tasks and drivers are backed up before they are removed. Duplicate files, large files, empty folders and startup shortcuts you remove go to the Recycle Bin.
  • Files the Cleaner deletes, wiped free space and erased drives can't be brought back.
  • Keep your own backups of anything important.
