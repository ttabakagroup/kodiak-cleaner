Install, Update and Uninstall on Windows

Install

  1. Download the Installer above.
  2. Double-click the .msi and follow the steps.

Portable: extract the Portable ZIP to any folder and run KodiakCleaner.exe.

Update

  • Installed: click Check Now under Settings > Updates, or turn on automatic updates there. You can also run a newer installer over the old one.
  • Portable: replace KodiakCleaner.exe and keep KodiakCleaner.ini and the Backups, Logs and Reports folders.

Your settings and backups are kept either way, and What's New shows what changed.

Uninstall

Uninstall Kodiak Cleaner from Windows Settings > Apps. It removes its scheduled cleanings, start-up entry, Recycle Bin menu item and settings, then asks whether to delete your backups, logs and reports too. Keep them if you might still want to undo a registry fix. For the portable version, just delete its folder.

Release Channels

Kodiak Cleaner is in the Dev channel (0.8.x). Beta (0.9.x) comes next, then Stable (1.x). Every channel installs to Program Files\Kodiak Cleaner, and updates stay on their own channel.
